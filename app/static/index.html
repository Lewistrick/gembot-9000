      How to Play

      You are one of up to 100 GemBot 9000 mining robots sharing a single underground mine. A brand-new mine opens every day, and whoever banks the most gems by midnight wins the day.

      Driving

      Keyboard: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ← / → turn (or W A S D). Space does a 180° about-face. PageUp / PageDown zoom, Enter chat, Tab players, R rails. Glance off a rock and you bounce (drill it by pushing straight in, see below). You can't hurt anyone: robots pass through each other.

      Touch: hold the left half of the screen to turn left, the right half to turn right, or both to go straight. Lift your fingers to brake. Tap the ↻ button to turn around.

      Gems & the base

      Drive over a gem to mine it. It goes into your cargo, and it's gone for everyone for the rest of the day. Cargo only scores once you bring it to the base (the dashed ring around the depot at the centre). Gems get richer, but rarer, the farther from the base you go. Mega gems glow amber, and show up the same way on the minimap.

      Drilling through walls

      You're a mining robot: hold forward into a wall and your drill bores through the tile in front of you. It takes about 1 second and burns 1 point of cargo; with no cargo it's free but takes 3 seconds, so you can always get out of a dead end. A drilled tile becomes floor for everybody (and for tracks and hubs) for the rest of the day. Let go of forward to stop. You can't drill inside the base.

      Rails: hubs, tracks and tolls

      Hauling gems home is slow, so build a rail. Stand still on an empty tile and tap the HUB button next to the turnaround button (or press B). It only shows up if you could pay for a hub. The possible tracks appear as transparent tracks leading to nearby hubs, each with its price: tap the one you want and the hub is built together with that track, or tap anywhere else to cancel. If only one track is possible, it is simply built (you're still asked if you'd have to pay from your score). A hub costs 25 and each tile of track 1. Tracks are straight (diagonals are fine), up to 60 tiles, and every tile must be floor.

      Park on a hub (or the base) and you can do exactly two things: SHIP your gems to the base on their own cart, which scores for you when it arrives, or switch on TRAVEL. Travel mode stays on and switches steering off (so a tap can't nudge you off the hub): tap any track leaving the hub you're on and you ride along it, and at the next hub you can tap again. Press TRAVEL again to switch it off (it also ends if you drive away from the hubs). Everyone shares every hub.

      Light: the mine is dark away from hubs. More than 100 tiles from the closest hub (the base counts) the screen starts to darken; at 250 it's pitch black and you're told that hubs are required to provide light; at 350 you're lost and sent back to the base with half your cargo. The lens and minimap still work in the dark. Build hubs to push the dark back.

      Paying: everything you buy is paid from your cargo first. If your cargo doesn't cover it you're asked whether to pay the missing amount from your banked gems (your score); nothing is taken from your score unless you agree. There's also a toll: each hub built by someone else at either end of your hop costs 1, paid to its builder (your own hubs and the base are free). Shipments pay their tolls out of the shipment itself.

      Day, rush hour & winners

      The day ends at midnight; cargo you haven't delivered is lost. In the last hour every gem is worth double. Yesterday's winner wears a crown, and the menu lists past winners.

      Chat & blocking

      Tap your own robot (or press Enter) to type a message; it floats above your robot until you change or clear it. Tap another robot to open its menu and block their chat bubbles. Tab (or tapping your score) opens a list of nearby players and today's top 10. Blocking only affects what you see.

      Your dashboard

      Three buttons next to the menu button help you find your way. The lens draws three arrows around your robot: yellow to the base, one in the colour of the gems to the nearest gem, and gray to the nearest hub, each with its distance in tiles. Click it again to hide them. The map button shows a minimap around you with walls, floor, hubs (gray) joined by their rails, the base (yellow) and the gems left to mine. The network button opens the whole rail network fullscreen with north up: every hub and track, the base marked BASE, and your robot as a white arrow marked YOU. Drag to pan, scroll (or pinch) to zoom, and leave with the cross in the corner (or Esc). Steering is paused while the map is open. The pane in the top-right shows the cargo you carry (it only counts once it's delivered to the base), your banked score and rank, and how far the base is.

      Staying in

      If you do nothing for 10 minutes you're removed to make room for others. Your score and cargo are kept for the day, and you can rejoin.
