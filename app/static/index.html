How to Play

You are GemBot 9000, a stubby little mining robot lowered into an endless underground mine. Buried in the dark rock are glittering gems — and digging them out is your whole reason for being. The further you rove from the entrance, the rarer and richer the seams become, and the rock itself slowly shifts colour the deeper you go.

Driving

Steer with the arrow keys: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ← / → turn. The whole mine rotates around you, so you are always driving toward the top of the screen. Ease off the throttle and you coast to a stop; steering bites harder the faster you go.

Rock

Solid rock blocks your way. Clip it at an angle and you scrape along it; hit it head-on and you stop dead. There's always another way around.

Mining gems

Gems grow as crystals embedded in the rock faces. Simply drive over one to mine it — its value (10–40) is added to your score and it crumbles into a little rubble. Once mined, it's gone for the rest of the run.

Your dashboard

The compass up top shows your heading, with your score and distance from the entrance to the right. The speedometer and battery sit along the bottom. Use PageUp / PageDown to zoom.

Goal

Mine as many gems as you can and beat your high score. Every day and every mode is a completely different mine, so there's always a new one to crack.
