      How to Play

      You are one of up to 100 GemBot 9000 mining robots sharing a single underground mine. A brand-new mine opens every day, and whoever banks the most gems by midnight wins the day.

      Driving

      Keyboard: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ← / → turn (or W A S D). Space does a 180° about-face. PageUp / PageDown zoom, Enter chat, Tab players, R rails. Glance off a rock and you bounce (drill it by pushing straight in, see below). You can't hurt anyone: robots pass through each other.

      Touch: hold the left half of the screen to turn left, the right half to turn right, or both to go straight. Lift your fingers to brake. Tap the ↻ button to turn around.

      Gems & the base

      Drive over a gem to mine it. It goes into your cargo, and it's gone for everyone for the rest of the day. Cargo only scores once you bring it to the base (the dashed ring around the depot at the centre). Gems get richer, but rarer, the farther from the base you go. Watch the radar for the glowing amber mega gems.

      Drilling through walls

      You're a mining robot: hold forward into a wall and your drill bores through the tile in front of you. It takes about 1 second and burns 1 point of cargo; with no cargo it's free but takes 3 seconds, so you can always get out of a dead end. A drilled tile becomes floor for everybody (and for tracks and hubs) for the rest of the day. Let go of forward to stop. You can't drill inside the base.

      Rails: hubs, tracks and tolls

      Hauling gems home is slow, so build a rail. Park on a hub (or the base) and two buttons appear beside the turnaround button: Rails and Track. Track lets you tap a spot: you'll see a straight line and its cost, and tapping the same spot again builds it. A new hub costs 25 and each tile of track costs 1, paid from your score. Tracks are straight (diagonals are fine), up to 60 tiles, and every tile must be floor. Tap an existing hub instead to link to it; loops aren't allowed, so every hub has one way home.

      The Rails button opens the network map. Tap a hub and ride there (very fast, either way), or ship your cargo to the base on its own cart, which scores for you when it arrives. Everyone shares every hub, and there's a toll: each hub built by someone else on your route costs 1 point of cargo, paid to its builder. Your own hubs are free. No cargo means no ride!

      You can also place a hub right where you stand still on an empty tile: tap the HUB button next to the turnaround button (or press B). You're offered the nearest hubs it can lead to, each with its price; pick one and the hub and its track are built together. A hub never exists without a track.

      Day, rush hour & winners

      The day ends at midnight; cargo you haven't delivered is lost. In the last hour every gem is worth double. Yesterday's winner wears a crown, and the menu lists past winners.

      Chat & blocking

      Tap your own robot (or press Enter) to type a message; it floats above your robot until you change or clear it. Tap another robot to open its menu and block their chat bubbles. Tab (or tapping your score) opens a list of nearby players and today's top 10. Blocking only affects what you see.

      Your dashboard

      The compass at the top doubles as a radar for gems and the way home. The pane in the top-right shows the cargo you carry (it only counts once it's delivered to the base), your banked score and rank, and how far the base is.

      Staying in

      If you do nothing for 10 minutes you're removed to make room for others. Your score and cargo are kept for the day, and you can rejoin.
