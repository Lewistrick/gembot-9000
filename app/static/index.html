      How to Play

      You are one of up to 100 GemBot 9000 mining robots sharing a single underground mine. A brand-new mine opens every day, and whoever banks the most gems by midnight wins the day.

      Driving

      Keyboard: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ← / → turn (or W A S D). Space does a 180° about-face. PageUp / PageDown zoom. Rocks bounce you off, but you can't hurt anyone: robots pass through each other.

      Touch: hold the left half of the screen to turn left, the right half to turn right, or both to go straight. Lift your fingers to brake. Tap the ↻ button to turn around.

      Gems & the base

      Drive over a gem to mine it. It goes into your cargo, and it's gone for everyone for the rest of the day. Cargo only scores once you bring it to the base (the dashed ring around the depot at the centre). Gems get richer, but rarer, the farther from the base you go. Watch the radar for the glowing amber mega gems.

      Chat & blocking

      Press Enter (or the speech-bubble button) and type a message; it floats above your robot until you change or clear it. Open the players panel (Tab or the two-heads button) to block someone's chat bubbles. It only affects what you see.

      Staying in

      If you do nothing for 10 minutes you're removed to make room for others. Your score and cargo are kept for the day, and you can rejoin.
